# Roadmap

This is the high-level phase roadmap: broad project outcomes and direction, not
a detailed work plan or coding-task list. The project owner controls phase
outcomes, priorities, and important requirements. Existing phase themes and
their provisional status below are preserved; do not treat them as approved
implementation scope or expand them without owner direction.

## Provisional Phases

| Phase | High-level outcome | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Project and documentation foundation | Complete |
| P1 | Reusable business ingredients, categories, menu products, and recipes | Provisional |
| P2 | Shopping, purchase history, and actual cost tracking | Provisional |
| P3 | Sales, expenses, and separate business/personal finance records | Provisional |
| P4 | AI assistance for recording and understanding business data | Provisional |
| P5 | Installable PWA and later sync, sign-in, hosting, and integrations | Provisional |

Detailed, reviewable phase breakdowns belong in
`docs/phases/<PHASE_ID>_PLAN.md`, created only when phase planning is requested.
They record proposal/approval state, owner-approved outcomes, work packages,
ordered coding tasks, dependencies, acceptance criteria, validation, and open
questions. The [task registry](TASK_REGISTRY.md) indexes approved work and
progress; it is not a substitute for owner approval. Task-specific Coding AI
prompts belong in `docs/prompts/<TASK_ID>.md` when generated for an approved
task and are linked from the plan/registry. Do not create speculative plan or
prompt files for future phases. The [Prompt AI workflow](PROMPT_AI_WORKFLOW.md)
defines intake and approval; unresolved product questions remain in
[decisions](DECISIONS.md#unresolved-questions).
