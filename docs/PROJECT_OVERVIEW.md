# Project Overview

## Purpose

KitchAI is an offline-first food business management web application for small
food production businesses.

## Intended Capabilities

The intended product scope includes business and personal finance records,
reusable ingredient and menu data, recipes, purchasing, actual cost tracking,
sales, expenses, and an AI assistant. These are product goals, not claims about
implemented functionality.

## Future Goals

Installable PWA support, multi-device synchronization, Google sign-in, Vercel
hosting, and integrations with QR ordering and delivery applications are
future goals. Their order, design, and feasibility are not yet settled.

## Status and Authority

The [project status](../PROJECT_STATUS.md) tracks implemented, verified, and
incomplete work. The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) is provisional; the
[decisions register](DECISIONS.md) distinguishes approved context from plans
and unresolved questions. Do not infer implementation or approval from this
overview.
